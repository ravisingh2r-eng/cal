What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measure of body fat based on height and weight that applies to adult men and women. It's a screening tool to identify possible weight problems.

BMI Calculation Formula

Metric: BMI = weight (kg) / [height (m)]²

Imperial: BMI = (weight (lbs) / [height (in)]²) × 703

Understanding Your BMI

  • Underweight (BMI < 18.5): May indicate malnutrition, eating disorder, or other health problems
  • Normal (BMI 18.5-24.9): Indicates healthy weight for your height
  • Overweight (BMI 25-29.9): May indicate you are overweight and should consider weight loss
  • Obese (BMI ≥ 30): Indicates obesity and increased health risks

Limitations of BMI

BMI is a useful screening tool but it has limitations. It doesn't account for:

  • Muscle mass - athletes may have high BMI but low body fat
  • Bone density
  • Overall body composition
  • Racial and sex differences
