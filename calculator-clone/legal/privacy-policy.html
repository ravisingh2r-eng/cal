Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 22, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Calculator Platform ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how we handle information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, use, and protection when you use our website and calculator tools.

By using our website, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

Calculator Inputs: When you use our calculators, the data you enter (such as loan amounts, income figures, health metrics) is processed locally in your browser and is NOT stored on our servers or transmitted to us.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with our website through Google Analytics, including:
    • Pages visited and calculators used
    • Time spent on pages
    • Device type and browser information
    • IP address (anonymized)
    • Referring website
  • Cookies: We use cookies and similar technologies for analytics and advertising purposes (see Section 4).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Analytics: To understand user behavior, improve our calculators, and optimize user experience
  • Advertising: To display relevant ads through Google AdSense
  • Performance: To monitor website performance and identify technical issues
  • Compliance: To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service

Important Note: All calculator computations are performed locally in your browser. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your calculation inputs or results.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

4.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Google Analytics cookies to understand usage patterns
  • Advertising Cookies: Google AdSense cookies to display personalized ads

4.2 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality and your user experience.

To opt-out of Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

To manage ad personalization: Google Ad Settings

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

5.1 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to analyze website traffic and user behavior. Google Analytics may collect:

  • Anonymized IP addresses
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited and interaction data

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

5.2 Google AdSense

We display ads through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show personalized ads based on your browsing history.

Learn more: How Google Uses Cookies in Advertising

6. Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • Client-Side Processing: All calculator operations are performed in your browser, ensuring your financial and personal data never leaves your device
  • HTTPS: Our website uses SSL/TLS encryption for secure connections
  • No Data Storage: We do not store your calculator inputs or results on our servers
  • Limited Collection: We only collect anonymized analytics data through trusted third-party services

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about data we collect
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (where applicable)
  • Opt-Out: Opt-out of analytics and personalized advertising
  • Data Portability: Request data in a portable format (where applicable)

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: privacy@yourdomain.com

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

9. International Users

Our website is operated from India and primarily serves Indian users. If you access our website from outside India, please be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in India.

9.1 GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For users in the European Union:

  • We process data based on legitimate interests (analytics, advertising)
  • You have the right to object to processing
  • You can withdraw consent for cookies at any time
  • We implement IP anonymization in Google Analytics

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Significant changes will be highlighted at the top of this page for at least 30 days.

11. Contact Information

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@yourdomain.com
  • Website: Calculator Platform

12. Consent

By using our website, you consent to this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our website.
